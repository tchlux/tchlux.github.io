Medical Plain Text Additional Disclosure

Last updated: April 16, 2026

Medical Plain Text is designed as a private, local-first iOS app for retrieving and formatting lab results.

Data accessed:
- Patient demographics needed to identify the connected chart
- Laboratory Observation resources
- DiagnosticReport resources related to lab results

Purpose of access:
- Retrieve recent lab results from the connected Epic/MyChart-compatible FHIR server
- Normalize lab values, units, timestamps, reference ranges, and abnormal flags
- Display lab rows in the app
- Generate deterministic plain-text output for the user's own copy/export workflow

Data storage:
- Retrieved data is intended to remain local to the user's device.
- OAuth access tokens are intended to be stored securely.
- The current development version may hold tokens only temporarily while the app is running.

Data sharing:
- The app does not intentionally send retrieved health data to a third-party analytics service.
- The app does not intentionally sell or rent user health data.
- The app does not intentionally upload health data to cloud storage.
- The app does not intentionally write data to Apple Health.
- If the user copies or exports text or JSON from the app, the user controls where that exported information goes.

Limitations:
- Data availability depends on the connected health system's FHIR configuration.
- Some records may be filtered, unavailable, delayed, corrected, or amended by the health system.
- The app should not be used for emergency care or time-sensitive medical decisions.
- Users should verify important results with the source health system and qualified clinicians.

Contact:
For questions about this private app, contact the app owner/operator directly.
