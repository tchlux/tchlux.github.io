Medical Plain Text Additional Disclosure

Last updated: April 21, 2026

Medical Plain Text is a local-first iPhone app for retrieving, organizing, and exporting a patient's own Epic/MyChart-compatible medical records in plain text.

Data accessed:
- Patient demographics needed to identify the connected chart
- Laboratory and vital-sign Observation resources
- DiagnosticReport resources, including narrative and attachment-bearing reports when available
- MedicationRequest, MedicationDispense, and Medication resources needed to show current and historical medication data
- Attachment metadata and previewable binary content when the connected health system makes it available

Purpose of access:
- Retrieve a patient's own records from the connected Epic/MyChart-compatible FHIR server
- Normalize values, units, timestamps, reference ranges, abnormal flags, report narratives, medication history, and attachment summaries
- Display records in the app for local reading, searching, and filtering
- Generate deterministic plain-text output for the user's own copy/export workflow

Data storage:
- Retrieved data is intended to remain local to the user's device.
- OAuth tokens and related auth state are stored securely in Keychain.
- Clinical data cached by the app is stored in an encrypted device-bound local store.

Data sharing:
- The app does not intentionally send retrieved health data to a third-party analytics service.
- The app does not intentionally sell or rent user health data.
- The app does not intentionally upload health data to cloud storage.
- The app does not intentionally write data to Apple Health.
- If the user copies or exports plain text or raw data from the app, the user controls where that exported information goes.

Limitations:
- Data availability depends on the connected health system's FHIR configuration.
- Some records, attachments, or previews may be filtered, unavailable, delayed, corrected, amended, or restricted by the health system.
- The app should not be used for emergency care or time-sensitive medical decisions.
- Users should verify important results with the source health system and qualified clinicians.
