Last updated: April 21, 2026

Medical Plain Text is a local-first iPhone application intended for personal use by the account holder. The app helps retrieve a patient's own Epic/MyChart-compatible medical records, store them locally on the user's device in encrypted form, and format them into plain text for the user's own review and export.

7. The app does not intentionally route data to Apple Health.
8. Use of the app depends on availability, permissions, and configuration of the connected Epic/MyChart FHIR server.
