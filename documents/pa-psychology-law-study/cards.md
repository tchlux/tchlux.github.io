### Question

### Choices

- B. The protected-title holdout changes the result; overlapping social-work practice may remain exempt, but offering services to the public under protected psychology titles is unlawful unless a statutory exemption applies

### Question
Certain photographs, X-rays, and tests are medically indicated after a child-abuse report. When should their summaries or reports be sent to the county agency?

### Choices
- A. Only if the alleged perpetrator requests them
- B. When the written report is sent, or within 48 hours after an electronic report, or as soon thereafter as possible
- C. Only after a court order is entered
- D. Never, because they remain privileged

### Rationale
They must be sent when the written report is sent, or within 48 hours after the electronic report, or as soon thereafter as possible, and they must also be made available to law enforcement in the course of investigation.

### Source Quote
Allows photographs, radiological exams, and other tests when clinically indicated and requires timely forwarding of summaries or reports to the county agency and availability to law enforcement.

### Question
A child is injured in County A, transported to County B for treatment, and dies in County B. Where must a mandated reporter send the abuse-related death report?

### Choices
- A. The coroner or medical examiner of County B, because death occurred there
- B. The coroner or medical examiner serving the county where the child lived
- C. The coroner or medical examiner of County A, because the child was transported to another county for treatment and the injuries were sustained in County A

Requires mandated reporters who suspect a child died from abuse to report to the appropriate coroner or medical examiner.

### Choices

- B. Consent is no defense, but impaired-professional diversion remains available if the former client initiated the relationship
- C. Consent is no defense, and the only remaining issue after 2 years is whether an immediate family member was involved
