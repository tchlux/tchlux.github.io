1. Start with [pa_psych_law_compact_top12_v1.md](/Users/thomaslux/Library/Mobile%20Documents/com~apple~CloudDocs/Documents/2026%20Alex%20PA%20Psych%20Test/drafts/pa_psych_law_compact_top12_v1.md), not the full `60`.
2. Give the evaluator only the question stems.
3. Do not attach source text, answer choices, or citations during the blind run.
4. Record whether the miss was:
   - wrong rule chosen
   - right rule, wrong detail
   - missed exception
   - deadline confusion
   - destination confusion
   - ambiguity caused by the item itself
5. If the miss is caused by ambiguity, rewrite the item.
6. If the miss is caused by a real neighboring-rule confusion, keep the item.
7. Only after the `Top 12` run should the rest of the `60` be tested.
