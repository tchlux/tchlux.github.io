## Act-Primary Items

### A1
- Sources: `A06`, `A08` | `Section 3`; `Section 3(3)`
- Question: An unlicensed social worker offers counseling that is consistent with social-work training and ethics, but advertises to the general public as a "psychologist." What changes the legal result: the overlapping professional training or the protected-title holdout?
- Answer: The protected-title holdout changes the result. Overlapping work by another recognized profession may remain exempt, but offering services to the public under protected psychology titles is unlawful unless a statutory exemption applies.
- Difficulty: `4/6 hard`
- Legitimacy: The stem supplies both the recognized-profession fact and the title-use fact, so the answer turns on the exemption boundary instead of an unstated assumption.

### A2
- Sources: `A07`
- Question: A licensed psychologist realizes a client needs treatment that falls outside the psychologist's competence. What duty does the Act impose beyond simply stopping the out-of-scope service?
