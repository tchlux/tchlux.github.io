# PA Psychology Law Fact-Flip Short Answer V1

Answer each with the exact rule outcome as briefly as possible.

## Q1

A mandated reporter who is a staff member of a facility suspects child abuse, makes an oral report by telephone to ChildLine, and later suspects the child died after transport to another county for treatment. What follow-up report is required after the telephone call, and to which county’s coroner or medical examiner must the death suspicion be reported?

## Q2

An impaired professional enrolls in an approved treatment program but refuses to enter the required Board agreement. What happens to the professional’s eligibility for the impaired-professional program, and what Board action is triggered?

## Q3

The Board temporarily suspends a psychologist’s license without a hearing. At the preliminary hearing held within the statutory period, the Board finds no prima facie case supporting the suspension. What must happen to the license?

## Q4

An out-of-state psychologist in good standing wants to practice in Pennsylvania on temporary assignment for 12 aggregate days. Is written notification to the Board required?

## Q5

A person satisfies the historical timing condition for the school-psychology private-practice grandfathering provision and is currently employed by a Pennsylvania school, but the person’s Educational Specialist certificate is no longer in good standing. Is the person eligible under the provision?
