# PA Psychology Law Hard MCQ V4

These questions are harder because every option is mostly right and differs by only one or two legally important clauses.

## Q1

Which statement is fully accurate?

A. A psychologist practicing on temporary assignment in Pennsylvania must always notify the Board first, may practice for up to 1 year, and may receive repeated 1-year extensions if endorsement remains pending.  
B. A psychologist practicing on temporary assignment for no more than 14 aggregate days is exempt from the notification requirement; otherwise temporary assignment requires written notice and written Board permission, lasts up to 6 months, and may be extended once for up to 6 more months.  
C. A provisional endorsement license is required before any out-of-state psychologist may perform even a 1-day temporary assignment in Pennsylvania, but once issued it may be renewed once every year without limit.  
D. Temporary assignment and provisional endorsement both last up to 6 months and both allow exactly one 6-month extension, but only provisional endorsement requires prior written Board permission.

## Q2

Which statement is fully accurate about child-abuse reporting in this scenario: a mandated reporter at a facility suspects abuse, makes an electronic report and receives confirmation, and also suspects the child later died from injuries sustained in another county after transport for treatment?

A. The reporter need not notify anyone at the facility once electronic confirmation is received, but must send a written follow-up report within 48 hours and notify the coroner where the child died.  
B. The reporter must immediately notify the person in charge or designated agent at the facility, need not make an additional oral or written child-abuse report after electronic confirmation, and must report the abuse-related death suspicion to the coroner or medical examiner of the county where the injuries were sustained.  
C. The reporter must notify the county agency in the county of death, and confidentiality bars any report to a coroner unless the family consents.  
D. The reporter must notify the facility and law enforcement, but electronic reporting does not eliminate the separate written report requirement.

## Q3

Which statement is fully accurate?

A. If more than 2 years have passed since therapy ended, sexual intimacies with a former client are automatically lawful, though the psychologist remains ineligible for impaired-professional diversion if discipline later occurs.  
B. After 2 years, sexual intimacies with a former client are allowed only if the former client consents in writing, and that written consent is a defense in disciplinary proceedings.  
C. After 2 years, the psychologist bears the burden of proving no exploitation in light of the listed factors; client consent is not a defense; and if the Board proceeds under the sexual-intimacy sections, impaired-professional diversion is unavailable.  
D. The Board bears the burden of proving exploitation, but if it succeeds the psychologist may still seek impaired-professional diversion instead of discipline.

## Q4

Which statement is fully accurate about Pennsylvania reporting and post-discipline duties?

A. A misdemeanor conviction and a final out-of-state disciplinary order must both be reported within 30 days, while an active suspension requires notice only to current clients within 90 days.  
B. A misdemeanor conviction must be reported within 30 days or at renewal, whichever is sooner; a final out-of-state disciplinary order must be reported within 90 days of notice or at renewal, whichever is sooner; and after active suspension the licensee must return the license and, within 30 days, notify current clients/patients and supervisees seeking licensure.  
C. A misdemeanor conviction is reported only at renewal, but a final out-of-state disciplinary order is reported within 90 days; active suspension requires return of the license only after appeals end.  
D. A final out-of-state disciplinary order is reported within 90 days, but if renewal falls first the licensee may wait until the later 90-day date; after active suspension the licensee must notify all former clients from the prior 5 years.

## Q5

Which statement is fully accurate about an impaired colleague in an approved treatment program who refuses to enter the required Board agreement?

A. Because the colleague is already in treatment, the agreement is optional; satisfactory progress alone preserves eligibility for deferred discipline.  
B. The treating psychologist loses the treatment-capacity reporting exemption once the colleague refuses the agreement, and must report immediately.  
C. The treating psychologist remains exempt from the subsection's mandatory-reporting duty by virtue of treatment capacity in an approved program, but the colleague's refusal to enter the agreement disqualifies the colleague from the impaired-professional program and activates an immediate Board investigation and disciplinary proceeding.  
D. Refusal to enter the agreement automatically suspends the colleague's license, but preserves eligibility for ultimate dismissal if treatment is later completed.
