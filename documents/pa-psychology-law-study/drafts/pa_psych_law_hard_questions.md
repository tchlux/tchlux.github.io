# PA Psychology Law Hard Questions

These are intentionally harder than the existing mock exam. They are designed to require combining multiple rules, exceptions, deadlines, or consequences.

## Q1

A Pennsylvania psychologist receives written notice on March 3 that another state entered a final disciplinary order against her. Pennsylvania later actively suspends her license on May 10, and she receives notice that day. What Pennsylvania reporting, return, and notification duties are triggered, and by when?

## Q2

A licensed psychologist who is a mandated reporter learns facts creating reasonable cause to suspect that a child was abused in County A, transported for treatment to County B, and died in County B. The psychologist worries that confidentiality, privilege, and the Mental Health Procedures Act bar disclosure. What report must be made, to whom, and what confidentiality rules change?

## Q3

Therapy ended 30 months ago. The psychologist then begins a sexual relationship with the former client. The former client clearly consented. In later disciplinary proceedings, what must the psychologist prove, what argument is unavailable, and can the psychologist seek placement in an impaired-professional program instead of discipline if the Board proceeds under the sexual-intimacy sections?

## Q4

An out-of-state psychologist in good standing wants to practice in Pennsylvania while full endorsement materials remain incomplete. On the first trip, the psychologist works in Pennsylvania for 13 aggregate days. Later, the psychologist wants to work for 7 months. After that, the Board issues a provisional endorsement license that later expires. State the notification or permission rules for the 13-day and 7-month periods, the duration and extension rules for temporary assignment, the duration and extension rules for the provisional endorsement license, and whether a second provisional license is available after expiration.

## Q5

A person enrolled in a school-psychology certification program on June 15, 2018, later obtains an Educational Specialist II certificate in good standing, but leaves school employment before opening a private practice. Under the Act and regulations, may the person privately perform acts permitted in the schools? Explain which conditions are satisfied and which are missing.

## Q6

A licensed psychologist employs an unlicensed person with graduate training in psychology. The employee prepares a report for a referring physician, and the practice bills for the service. What signatures, disclosures, and status communications are required, and who retains ultimate professional responsibility?

## Q7

A psychologist is treating an impaired colleague inside an approved treatment program. The colleague refuses to enter the Board agreement under which the license would be suspended or revoked subject to a stay during satisfactory participation. Must the treating psychologist report the colleague under the impaired-professional mandatory-reporting subsection, and what happens to the colleague's eligibility for the impaired-professional program?

## Q8

Compare these two cases. Case A: the Board has probable cause that a psychologist presents an immediate and clear danger because of current impairment, and the psychologist refuses a compelled examination. Case B: a licensee is convicted of a Pennsylvania controlled-substance felony. For each case, identify the suspension mechanism, hearing or stay rules, and any special evidentiary effect of refusal.

## Q9

A licensee is convicted of a misdemeanor on April 1 and later receives notice on April 20 that another state entered a final disciplinary order. When must each event be reported to the Board?

## Q10

A mandated reporter employed by a facility makes an immediate electronic child-abuse report and receives electronic confirmation from the Department. What additional internal or follow-up reporting step is still required, and what additional oral or written report is no longer required?
