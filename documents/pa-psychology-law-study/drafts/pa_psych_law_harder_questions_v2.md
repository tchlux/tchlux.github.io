# PA Psychology Law Harder Questions V2

These are second-pass questions refined after a blind `mini` run. They target the exact failure modes the first run exposed: wrong deadlines, collapsed regimes, and missed exceptions.

## Q1

A psychologist receives notice on March 3 that another state entered a final disciplinary order. The psychologist's Pennsylvania biennial renewal is due April 15. By what date must the other-state discipline be reported to Pennsylvania, and why?

## Q2

A mandated reporter suspects child abuse caused a child's death. The child was injured in County A, transported to County B for treatment, and died in County B. The reporter already made an electronic child-abuse report and received confirmation from the Department. What additional report or notification duties still remain, and to whom?

## Q3

An out-of-state psychologist in good standing practices in Pennsylvania for 12 aggregate days without contacting the Board. Months later, the same psychologist wants to practice for 20 more aggregate days, still without full Pennsylvania licensure. Which period is exempt from Board notification, what had to occur before the later period could lawfully begin, and what is the maximum temporary-assignment duration if the Board grants permission and one extension?

## Q4

A psychologist is acting in a treatment capacity to an impaired colleague in an approved treatment program. The colleague then refuses to enter the Board agreement under which the license would be suspended or revoked subject to a stay during satisfactory participation. Does the treatment-capacity reporting exemption still matter for the treating psychologist, and what immediate consequence follows for the colleague?

## Q5

A Pennsylvania licensee is convicted of a misdemeanor on April 1, receives notice on April 20 that another state entered a final disciplinary order, and then receives notice on May 10 that Pennsylvania has actively suspended the license. List the separate Pennsylvania deadlines and duties triggered by each event.
