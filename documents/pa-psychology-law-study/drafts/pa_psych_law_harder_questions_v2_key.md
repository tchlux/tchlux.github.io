# PA Psychology Law Harder Questions V2 Key

## Q1

The discipline must be reported by April 15, not by 90 days after March 3, because the rule is `within 90 days or on the biennial renewal application, whichever is sooner`.

Sources: `Section 8.1`, `§ 41.91(b)`

## Q2

Two duties remain. First, the reporter must notify the person in charge of the facility or the designated agent after making the report. Second, because abuse is suspected to have caused death and the child was transported for treatment, the reporter must report that suspicion to the coroner or medical examiner of County A, where the injuries were sustained. No additional oral or written child-abuse report to DHS/ChildLine is required after electronic reporting plus confirmation.

Sources: `§ 41.71(b)-(c)`, `§ 41.73`

## Q3

The 12-day aggregate period is exempt from the temporary-assignment notification requirement. Before the later 20-day period could lawfully begin, the psychologist had to give written notification to the Board and receive written temporary permission to practice. If granted, temporary assignment can last 6 months with no more than one extension of up to 6 additional months, for a 12-month maximum temporary-assignment period.

Sources: `§ 41.52(c)`

## Q4

Yes. The treatment-capacity exemption still matters because a person acting in treatment capacity in an approved program is exempt from the subsection's mandatory-reporting duty. But refusal to enter the Board agreement immediately disqualifies the colleague from the impaired-professional program and activates an immediate Board investigation and disciplinary proceeding.

Sources: `Section 18(c)`, `Section 18(f)`

## Q5

The misdemeanor conviction must be reported within 30 days of April 1 or on renewal, whichever is sooner. The other-state final disciplinary order must be reported within 90 days of receiving notice on April 20 or on renewal, whichever is sooner. Once Pennsylvania actively suspends the license on May 10, the licensee must return the suspended license to the Board and, within 30 days of receiving notice of the Pennsylvania discipline, notify all current clients/patients and all supervisees seeking licensure from the licensee in writing, with the required notice contents.

Sources: `§ 41.91(a)-(b)`, `Section 17`, `§ 41.92`
