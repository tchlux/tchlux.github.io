# PA Psychology Law Rewrite Checklist V1

Use this before promoting a revised item into the compact set.

1. Does the stem supply every fact needed to choose the rule?
2. Would one changed fact likely flip the answer?
3. Is the likely wrong answer a real nearby rule rather than noise?
4. If the item is short-answer, does it still avoid being purely recall-based?
5. If the item is multiple-choice or multi-select, do the options leak the structure too easily?
6. Does the answer stay unique without the citation being shown?
7. Does the item preserve the intended source-family balance?
8. If the item replaces an older prompt, is the improvement visible on `R`, `C`, `F`, or `L` rather than just in wording style?
