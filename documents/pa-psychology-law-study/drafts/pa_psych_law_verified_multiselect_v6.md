## Q1

Choose all fully supported statements about Pennsylvania reporting and post-discipline notice duties.

A. A licensee convicted of a misdemeanor must notify the Board within 30 days of the conviction or on the biennial renewal application, whichever is sooner.  
B. The written notice required after active suspension or revocation must include the sanction imposed, the effective date and length of the sanction, the nature of the violation, and a statement that the licensee will assist patients in obtaining alternative professional resources and in transferring psychological records.  
C. A licensee whose Pennsylvania license has been actively suspended must return the suspended or revoked license to the Board and, within 30 days of receiving notice of the disciplinary action, notify all current clients/patients and any individuals obtaining supervision for licensure from the licensee in writing.  
D. A licensee who receives notice of a final disciplinary order from another jurisdiction must notify the Board within 90 days of receiving notice or on the biennial renewal application, whichever is later.  

## Q2

Choose all fully supported statements about this scenario: a mandated reporter working at a facility suspects child abuse, makes an electronic report and receives confirmation, and later has reasonable cause to suspect the child died after being transported to another county for treatment.

A. The death suspicion must be reported to the coroner or medical examiner of the county where the child died.  
B. The reporter must report immediately and then immediately notify the person in charge of the facility or the designated agent of that person.  
C. The electronic confirmation relieves the reporter of the duty to make an additional oral or written child-abuse report.  
D. If clinically indicated, photographs, radiological examinations, or other medical tests may be taken, and the resulting medical summaries or reports must be sent to the county children and youth social service agency when the written report is sent, or within 48 hours after the electronic report, or as soon thereafter as possible, and made available to law enforcement in the course of investigation.  

## Q3

Choose all fully supported statements about temporary assignment practice by an out-of-state psychologist.

A. The Board may grant repeated 6-month extensions so long as the psychologist remains in good standing in the original jurisdiction.  
B. A psychologist on temporary assignment in Pennsylvania for an aggregate of no more than 14 days is exempt from the notification requirement.  
C. Outside the short-assignment exception, temporary assignment may continue for up to 6 months only after written notification to the Board and written temporary permission from the Board.  
D. Requests for extension must be made in writing, and the Board may grant no more than one extension that does not exceed an additional 6 months.  

## Q4

Choose all fully supported statements about Pennsylvania sexual-intimacy rules.

A. Once 2 years have passed after termination of the professional relationship, sexual intimacies with a former client may occur only in very limited circumstances, and the former client’s consent may be raised as a defense if the psychologist shows no exploitation.  
B. A psychologist may not accept as a client/patient a person with whom the psychologist has engaged in sexual intimacies.  
C. In disciplinary proceedings under these sections, the former client’s consent is not a defense, and the psychologist bears the burden of proving no exploitation in light of the listed factors.  
D. If the Board takes disciplinary or corrective action for conduct prohibited by these sections, the psychologist is not eligible for placement into an impaired-professional program in lieu of disciplinary or corrective action.  

## Q5

Choose all fully supported statements about the impaired-professional provisions.

A. If the professional refuses to enter the agreement but keeps participating in treatment, the professional remains eligible for the program and no immediate investigation is triggered.  
B. The Board may defer and ultimately dismiss corrective action for an impaired professional who is progressing satisfactorily in an approved treatment program, but that protection does not apply to a professional convicted of a felonious act under the Pennsylvania Controlled Substance, Drug, Device and Cosmetic Act or a felony relating to a controlled substance in the United States or another jurisdiction.  
C. An impaired professional who enrolls in an approved treatment program must enter an agreement with the Board under which the license will be suspended or revoked, though enforcement may be stayed while the professional remains in the program, makes satisfactory progress, complies with the agreement, and follows any Board-imposed practice limitations.  
D. A person or facility acting in a treatment capacity to an impaired professional in an approved treatment program is exempt from the mandatory-reporting requirement of that subsection.  

## Q6

Choose all fully supported statements comparing temporary and automatic suspension.

A. A temporary suspension remains in effect until final Board action with no maximum duration.  
B. Temporary suspension may be imposed without a hearing when the Board determines there is an immediate and clear danger to public health and safety, but the Board must then commence formal action and hold a preliminary hearing within 30 days to determine whether a prima facie case supports the suspension.  
C. If the Board determines at the preliminary hearing that no prima facie case exists, the suspended license must be immediately restored.  
D. Automatic suspension occurs upon legal commitment because of mental incompetence or upon specified controlled-substance felony convictions, and the automatic suspension is not stayed pending appeal of the conviction.  

## Q7

Choose all fully supported statements about the school-psychology private-practice grandfathering provision.

A. A previously issued Educational Specialist certificate is enough even if it is no longer valid or in good standing.  
B. The gateway timing condition is met if, on or before June 30, 2017, the person completed all requirements for certification as an Educational Specialist I or II in school psychology, or if the person was enrolled in a program leading to that certification on or before June 30, 2018.  
C. Even if the timing condition is met, the person must be employed by a public or private school in Pennsylvania or by a facility or educational program regulated by the Department of Education.  
D. Even if the timing and employment conditions are met, the person must still hold a valid Educational Specialist I or II certificate in school psychology issued by the Department of Education that is in good standing.  

## Q8

Choose all fully supported statements about reactivation after biennial-registration lapse or inactivity.

A. The late renewal fee is waived for periods of nonpractice, but it still applies for periods in which the applicant practiced only in exempt settings.  
B. A person seeking reactivation after a license became inactive or lapsed because of failure to register biennially must apply on Board forms, pay the renewal fee for the current biennium, show compliance with continuing education requirements including the child-abuse training requirement, and submit a notarized affidavit identifying periods of nonpractice or practice in exempt settings.  
C. Once the applicant pays the late and renewal fees, the Board may still require renewal compliance, but it may not take disciplinary action based on the unauthorized practice.  
D. If the applicant engaged in unauthorized practice in Pennsylvania while inactive or lapsed, the applicant must pay a late renewal fee of $5 for each month or part of a month of unauthorized practice, in addition to biennial renewal fees for bienniums during which the unauthorized practice occurred.
