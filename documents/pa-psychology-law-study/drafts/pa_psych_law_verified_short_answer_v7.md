# PA Psychology Law Verified Short Answer V7

Answer each with the exact rule outcome as briefly as possible.

## Q1

## Q2

A mandated reporter working as a staff member of a facility suspects child abuse, makes an electronic report, receives confirmation, and later has reasonable cause to suspect the child died after transport to another county for treatment. What additional notification and report are still required, and to whom?

## Q3

## Q4

## Q5

May the person use the private-practice grandfathering provision?
