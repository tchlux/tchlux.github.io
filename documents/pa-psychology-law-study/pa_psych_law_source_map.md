# PA Psychology Law Source Map

This source map creates exactly 120 chunked source units for a Pennsylvania psychology law mock exam:

- `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf`: `A01-A40` (40 chunks)
- `pa-state-board-of-psychology-regulations.pdf`: `B01-B80` (80 chunks)

## pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf

### Section 1 and Section 1.1
- `A01` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 1; Section 1.1` | `scope/licensure` | Declares that the practice of psychology affects public safety and welfare, should be regulated in the public interest, liberally construed, and is titled the Professional Psychologists Practice Act.

### Section 2. Definitions
- `A02` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 2` | `scope/licensure` | Defines the practice of psychology broadly as compensated application of psychological principles to evaluation, counseling, adjustment, diagnosis, treatment, remediation, consultation, and related services.
- `A03` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 2(ii)(a)` | `scope/licensure` | Defines measuring and testing as psychological assessment or evaluation of abilities, attitudes, aptitudes, achievements, adjustments, motives, personality dynamics, and other attributes through standardized measures or other recognized methods.
- `A04` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 2(ii)(b)` | `scope/licensure` | Defines psychological methods as interpersonal application of learning and motivation principles to modify perception, adjustment, feelings, values, self-concept, goals, and adaptive patterns.
- `A05` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 2(ii)(c)` | `scope/licensure` | Defines psychological consulting as interpreting or reporting psychological science or theory, rendering expert psychological opinion or evaluation, or engaging in applied psychological research.

### Section 3. Necessity for License
- `A06` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 3` | `scope/licensure` | Makes it unlawful to practice, offer to practice, or hold oneself out by psychology-based titles without a license, subject to stated exemptions.
- `A07` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 3(2)` | `supervision/practice limits` | Exempts other healing arts licensees, prevents this act from authorizing psychologists to practice other healing arts, and requires psychologists to help clients obtain qualified care for issues outside their competence.
- `A08` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 3(3)` | `scope/licensure` | Preserves work of qualified members of other recognized professions and volunteers in crisis or emergency situations when the work is consistent with their own training and ethics.
- `A09` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 3(4); Section 3(4.1)` | `supervision/practice limits` | Exempts specified federal, correctional, human-services, and school-based psychology work done as part of normal employment and permits DOC or DHS employees to perform testing and assessment under licensed-psychologist review and cosignature.
- `A10` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 3(6)` | `supervision/practice limits` | Exempts faculty, staff, students, interns, residents, and researchers in accredited settings when work is part of their ordinary role or supervised course of study and trainee titles clearly indicate training status.
- `A11` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 3(7); Section 3(7.1)` | `endorsement/mobility` | Allows temporary assignment practice for persons meeting minimum qualifications and authorizes temporary licenses for substantially equivalent out-of-state psychologists, subject to board discretion.
- `A12` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 3(8); Section 3(9)` | `supervision/practice limits` | Permits internal business and industrial personnel applications and clerical or administrative support under supervision, while prohibiting sale of public psychological services without properly licensed providers.
- `A13` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 3(10.1)` | `supervision/practice limits` | Grandfathers certain school psychologists for private practice if they timely completed or entered qualifying certification pathways, hold valid specialist certification, and work in qualifying educational settings.
- `A14` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 3(11); Section 3(12)` | `supervision/practice limits` | Permits licensed psychologists to employ postdoctoral trainees and graduate-trained employees only under full direction, control, and supervision according to board regulations.

### Section 3.1. State Board of Psychology
- `A15` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 3.1(a)` | `board powers/admin` | Establishes the nine-member board, including citizenship and residency requirements, two public members, six licensed psychologists, and the commissioner as the ninth member.
- `A16` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 3.1(b); Section 3.1(c); Section 3.1(i)` | `board powers/admin` | Sets member terms, vacancies, quorum rules, physical-attendance voting limits, and a minimum of six meetings per year.
- `A17` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 3.1(d)-(h)` | `board powers/admin` | Covers annual officer selection, per diem and expenses, sunset review, and forfeiture rules for missed meetings or public-member training seminars.

### Section 3.2. Powers of the Board
- `A18` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 3.2` | `board powers/admin` | Grants authority over applicant qualifications, regulations, ethical standards, licensing decisions, hearings, complaints, prosecution, credential evaluation, and examination waivers.

### Section 3.3. Fees, Fines and Civil Penalties
- `A19` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 3.3` | `board powers/admin` | Allows board-set fees by regulation, bureau fee increases when needed for enforcement, deposit of fees and penalties into the augmentation account, and fees for exams, renewals, certifications, licensures, and applications.

### Section 3.4. Reports of the Board
- `A20` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 3.4` | `board powers/admin` | Requires annual reporting of financial needs and budget requests and annual legislative reporting on complaint types, case status, board action, and time to resolution.

### Section 6. Qualifications for License
- `A21` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 6(a)(1)-(4)` | `qualifications/exams` | Requires acceptable moral character, qualifying doctoral education and supervised experience, passage of the board examination, and payment of required fees.
- `A22` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 6(a)(5)` | `qualifications/exams` | Bars licensure after specified controlled-substance felonies unless 10 years have elapsed, rehabilitation is shown, and all other qualifications are met.
- `A23` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 6(b); Section 6(c)` | `qualifications/exams` | Requires sworn truthfulness in applications with perjury exposure and permits a second examination after failure, with later eligibility governed by board regulation.

### Section 8. Refusal, Suspension or Revocation of License
- `A24` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 8(a)(1)-(5)` | `discipline/enforcement` | Authorizes discipline for failure to meet licensure standards, misleading practice representations, fraud in obtaining a license, gross incompetence or misconduct, and false biennial registration.
- `A25` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 8(a)(6); Section 8(a)(7)` | `discipline/enforcement` | Authorizes discipline for felony convictions, certain practice-related misdemeanors, specified diversionary dispositions, and disciplinary action by another jurisdiction.
- `A26` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 8(a)(8)` | `discipline/enforcement` | Authorizes discipline for inability to practice safely due to impairment, permits compelled mental or physical examinations, treats unjustified refusal as an admission, and allows later proof of recovery.
- `A27` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 8(a)(9)-(15)` | `discipline/enforcement` | Authorizes discipline for violating regulations or orders, aiding unlicensed practice, immoral or unprofessional conduct, coercive solicitation, failing statutory duties, false third-party billing, and inadequate records.
- `A28` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 8(b); Section 8(c)` | `discipline/enforcement` | Lists available sanctions from denial through probation and corrective treatment while preserving notice, hearing, adjudication, and appeal rights.
- `A29` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 8(d)` | `discipline/enforcement` | Allows immediate temporary suspension when there is an immediate and clear danger, followed by a prompt prima facie hearing with defined participation rights and a 180-day maximum duration.
- `A30` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 8(e)` | `discipline/enforcement` | Provides automatic suspension upon mental incompetency commitment or specified controlled-substance felony convictions, without a stay pending appeal.

### Section 8.1. Reporting of Multiple Licensure
- `A31` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 8.1` | `notice/reporting` | Requires reporting of multiple licensure on biennial renewal and reporting of other-jurisdiction discipline within 90 days or at renewal, whichever is sooner.

### Section 11. Penalties and Injunctions Against Unlawful Practice
- `A32` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 11(a)` | `discipline/enforcement` | Makes unlicensed practice and other violations criminal, with misdemeanor penalties for a first offense and increased penalties for additional offenses.
- `A33` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 11(b); Section 11(c)` | `discipline/enforcement` | Allows civil penalties up to $1,000 after hearing and authorizes injunctions against unlawful practice without proof of individual injury.

### Section 11.1. Subpoenas
- `A34` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 11.1` | `discipline/enforcement` | Authorizes investigative subpoenas, witness oaths, document production, and Commonwealth Court enforcement, while protecting patient records absent consent or court order showing necessity.

### Section 12. Out-of-State Licensing
- `A35` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 12` | `endorsement/mobility` | Allows licensure without examination for holders of substantially equivalent out-of-state psychology licenses or certificates.

### Section 14. Severability
- `A36` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 14` | `discipline/enforcement` | Makes the act severable so invalidation of one part does not invalidate the remainder.

### Section 15. Renewal; Fees; Records
- `A37` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 15` | `renewal/continuing education` | Provides for biennial renewal, board-set continuing education, no credit for office-management or practice-building courses, and public licensure records kept by the commissioner and board.

### Section 16. Reinstatement of License
- `A38` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 16` | `discipline/enforcement` | Bars reinstatement of revoked licenses except by court order or after at least five years with full relicensure qualifications, including the examination requirement.

### Section 17. Surrender of Suspended or Revoked License
- `A39` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 17` | `discipline/enforcement` | Requires return of suspended or revoked licenses or registrations as directed by the board, with failure constituting a third-degree misdemeanor.

### Section 18. Impaired Professional
- `A40` | `pa-professional-psychologists-practice-act.pdf` | `Section 18` | `discipline/enforcement` | Establishes the impaired-professional program, consultant role, deferred discipline for compliant participants, required agreements, provider disclosures, mandatory impairment reporting, immunity, and fines for nonreporting.
