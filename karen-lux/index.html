In loving memory

Karen Holm Lux

November 11, 1965 – June 21, 2026

Remembering Karen

Karen found joy in time with her children, grandchildren, and extended family; in her river and country homes; and in trips to the Outer Banks and Blue Ridge. Proud of her Danish heritage, she cherished the warmth and togetherness of hygge. Her love, laughter, patience, and courage continue to inspire those who knew her.
