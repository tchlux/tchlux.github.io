About Thomas Lux

Thomas Lux is the developer behind Medical Data App. When Epic shows the business name “Thomas Lux,” it is referring to the individual developer of this project.

Background

Thomas Lux is a software engineer and AI / machine learning researcher. His work centers on high-performance code, mathematical modeling, interpretable models, optimization, and practical systems that work well with limited data.

He received a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Virginia Tech in 2020 and a B.S. in Computer Science from Roanoke College, with minors in Mathematics and Physics.

Why This App Exists

Medical Data App is a free-time, volunteer-built project. It was created to help people retrieve, read, search, and copy their own medical data in plain text from supported Epic / MyChart connections.

This is an independent open-source project. It is not affiliated with Epic, MyChart, or any hospital or health system.
