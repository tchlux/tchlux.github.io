About Thomas Lux

Hello! I am the creator of Medical Plain Text. When Epic shows the business name “Thomas Lux,” it is referring to my developer account I used to create this project. To be abundantly clear, I do not see or access any data personally, only the application on your device sees and stores your data.

Background

I'm a software engineer and AI / machine learning researcher. My education and work centers on high-performance code, training language models, mathematical modeling, interpretable models, optimization, and building practical applied systems in the real world.

I received a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Virginia Tech in 2020 and my B.S. in Computer Science from Roanoke College in 2016, with minors in Mathematics and Physics.

Why This App Exists

Medical Plain Text is a volunteer effort project. I originally created it out of a need for easier access to MyChart data for myself and my immediate family members. But I hope that it can be helpful for anyone who wants to retrieve, read, search, and copy their own medical data in plain text from supported Epic / MyChart connections.

This is an independent open-source project. It is not affiliated with Epic, MyChart, or any hospital or health system.
